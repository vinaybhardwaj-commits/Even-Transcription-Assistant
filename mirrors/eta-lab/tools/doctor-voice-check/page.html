How to check

Link cards show a room, a date and the doctor we think it is, with the evidence in plain words and short clips of that voice from different consults, plus one clip of someone else in the room for contrast. Listen, then Agree if the clips are that doctor's voice, Disagree if not (then say who it is, or Unknown), or Not sure. Same person? cards give two clips: say whether they are the same person. Play any clip as often as you like. Your answers save as you go and only you see them here.

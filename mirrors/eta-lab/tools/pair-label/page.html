Each item is the same stretch of speech transcribed two ways, A and B, with the language the system claims for it. Read both and say which one you could follow better. You are judging the text, not the doctor, and the order of A and B carries no meaning.

What the five answers mean

A is better or B is better: that version reads more like real speech in the claimed language and you could follow it more easily. About the same: both read equally well, or equally badly. Neither is readable: both are garbled, empty or in the wrong language. Unsure: you cannot tell. Your answers save as you go and only you see them here. On a computer, keys 1 to 5 choose the answer, N or the right arrow moves on and P or the left arrow goes back.

Each card is one whole EHRC OPD consult. A surgical conversation is one where the doctor names a specific procedure and describes what it involves or how it will affect the patient; a keyword alone does not count. Naming a procedure and describing it are two separate questions: a doctor can describe one without saying its name.

How to review

Jev has read the consult and proposes answers. Nothing is pre-selected: read the transcript, then press Agree or Correct on each answer. Questions that don't apply are hidden, and they appear if your answers make them relevant. Evidence chips jump to the turns Jev used. When a consult has full audio, use the player at the top: tap any time stamp in the transcript to hear from there. Greyed turns are outside the consult and are not scored. If the transcript is too garbled to judge, say so. Your answers save as you go and only you see them here. Please don't copy patient text out of this page.

Loading the consults…

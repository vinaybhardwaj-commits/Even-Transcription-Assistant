Calibration 30 — hear it, read it, judge it

30 items: 20 single transcript lines and 10 A/B pairs. Play the audio for each item (the ▶ button hears exactly that line, ~1 s of padding each side). Toggle English only if you want a literal gloss; the first judgement is your read of the text. "Can't judge this language" skips the item for you without counting it. Your answers save as you go; only you see them.

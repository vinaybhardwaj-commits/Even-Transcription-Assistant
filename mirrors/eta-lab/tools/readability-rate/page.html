How to rate

Readable: could you follow what was said? Useful: would this text help you understand the consult if you had not been there? Wrong language: are lines shown in a language or script that is not what was spoken? Garbled: words that make no sense, endless repeats, or invented-sounding text. Choose the closest answer; add a note if something specific stands out. Your ratings save as you go and only you see them here. On a computer, keys 1 to 3 answer the first open question, N or the right arrow moves on and P or the left arrow goes back.

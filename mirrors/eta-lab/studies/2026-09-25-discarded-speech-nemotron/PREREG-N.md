## Measure and reading rule (fixed now)
Per window: Nemotron speech seconds (union of turns) / measured seconds = `nemo_frac`. A window is "speech-bearing by Nemotron" if `nemo_frac >= 0.20` (the same 20 % of measured seconds split-speaker's rule uses; here on diarized speech alone, no ASR).
- Controls must separate before the 30 are read: guard-accepted windows speech-bearing in >= 7 of 10 AND noisy no-text windows speech-bearing in <= 1 of 14 (same thresholds as the frozen rule). If they do not, the refused-window counts from arm N are NOT to be read (reported as such).
- If they separate: K_N = number of the 30 refused windows speech-bearing by Nemotron, reported next to split-speaker's arm-D diarizer-only count and its full decision; agreement or disagreement per window is listed by id. No decision is taken from arm N; the frozen rule decides.
## Limits stated in advance
A diarizer flags speech-like sound (it can fire on hum/noise; Nemotron and pyannote fail differently), so `speech-bearing` is an upper-bound signal, not proof of intelligible speech; 30 windows bound a rate, they do not measure it; chunks are <= 60 s cuts of the discarded spans (context is short); base Nemotron under-counts minor speakers, which does not matter here (speech vs no speech, not counts).

# SURGICAL-CHAIN-LINKER P1: pre-registration v7.7 (linker, 24 Sep 2026): the Rx-side margin is measured on consult-localized text; the pilot items are excluded from accuracy claims

Addendum to v7.6; nothing earlier is edited. Written after the **first mechanics pilot** (ruling 191) on the 4 items whose shortlisted rooms all have production text. The pilot is not a result and its verdicts are not reported per class (the class key was never read). It ran end to end: 45 files de-identified on the GPU, all passed the residual-identifier gate, 41 Jev calls in 46 seconds.

## K1. What the pilot showed (mechanics only)
- **Verdicts under v7.6 (window consult plus or minus 10 minutes for everything):** 1 accepted (clean: best room 0.94, the other two 0.02 and 0.04, best decoy 0.31); 1 ambiguous tie on the prescription side (best room 0.90, but a same-day decoy prescription scored **0.95** against the same transcript); 2 below the floor (one item's single shortlisted room scored 0.04; another item's three shortlisted rooms scored 0.05, 0.07, 0.05, so its consult is not found in any of them).
- **The tie has a timing cause I can check without reading text:** in that item exactly one same-day decoy visit starts **inside the padded window** (15.6 minutes after the consult start, 6 minutes after it ended). Of the other three items, one has a decoy inside the window and two have none; the only tie is the item with a neighbouring visit inside the padding. This is evidence, not proof (I did not read the text).
- **Why it is structural.** The pre-registered decoy is the same doctor's OTHER same-day prescription scored against the SAME transcript. The window (consult plus or minus 10 minutes) is wide enough to contain the doctor's adjacent patient. That patient's prescription then matches the same window, so the own-versus-decoy margin fails by construction for any visit whose neighbour falls in the padding.

## K2. The rule change, fixed now
1. **Room choice is unchanged:** the floor (best room's own-prescription score >= 0.50) and the room margin (best room beats the next by >= 0.15) are computed on the pre-registered consult plus or minus 10 minute window text.
2. **The prescription-side margin is computed on consult-localized text:** the turns whose timestamp lies within the consult's own window (queue `called_at` to `completed_at`, or the capped prescription window) **plus or minus 2 minutes**, in the best room. Own prescription and every same-day decoy are scored against that localized text; the margin is own minus the best decoy, >= 0.15 to pass. If the localized text is empty the item is `INCOMPLETE`, not rejected.
3. **The verdict names** stay: `ACCEPTED`, `AMBIGUOUS_TIE(room)` and `AMBIGUOUS_TIE(rx)` (never forced; reported as their own count, ruling 191), `REJECT_BELOW_FLOOR` (read as "not linked in the shortlisted rooms": the shortlist is a gate, and a consult can be absent from all of them), `SINGLE` and `INCOMPLETE`.
4. **The 4 pilot items are excluded from every accuracy or rate claim of link 3.** They informed this rule, so scoring them under it would be tune-then-score. They are reported separately as the pilot; the primary analysis runs on the other 33 items. A second mechanics run of the pilot items under K2 is allowed and is reported as mechanics only.

## K3. Mechanics findings from the pilot (no rule)
A locale problem in the de-identification subprocess (an ASCII locale crashed the writer on the first non-ASCII character, leaving partial output) is fixed by forcing UTF-8 mode and by refusing to score unless every output parses and keeps its turn count. Room windows carry 65 to 706 turns (2,700 to 15,800 characters); one shortlisted room had only 7 turns (a nearly silent room, not missing text).

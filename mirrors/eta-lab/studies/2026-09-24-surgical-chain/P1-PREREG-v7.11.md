# SURGICAL-CHAIN-LINKER P1: pre-registration v7.11 (linker, 24 Sep 2026): language is not random across classes: per-class LANGUAGE_LIMITED counts and an English-share covariate (refuter #811)

Addendum to v7.10; nothing earlier is edited. Report-only additions; no rule changes. Code: `p1/link3_run.py` (the covariate) and `p1/link3_tables.py` (the pre-registered table set, run **only** when all 37 items are in and the 10 windows of the four fully covered items are finished; it is the one step that joins results to the sealed class key). No class join has been made.

## O1. LANGUAGE_LIMITED per class and per doctor
Every link-3 table set reports the `LANGUAGE_LIMITED` count **per class** (positives, split A+ full and A+ partial; same-week N2; N1) and **per doctor** (pseudonymised d01, d02, ...). If one class is more often non-English, the surviving class comparison is confounded by language; the per-class split tells Fable whether the loss is only a yield loss or also a bias. If `LANGUAGE_LIMITED` is large, a translation step is Fable's call; nothing in P1 assumes it.

## O2. English-share covariate on every scored item
For every item that is scored (not withheld), carry its **English share**: the share of turns that are English in (a) the best room's plus or minus 10 minute window and (b) the localized consult window. A code-switched consult scored on its English turns alone has less of the complaint to match, so its own score can fall for a language reason rather than an absent visit. Report the **accept rate by English-share band of the best room: below 70%, 70 to 90%, above 90%**, with the n in every cell, and the below-floor share by band. No accept rate is quoted without this table beside it.

## O3. The pre-registered table set (fixed now; produced only by `link3_tables.py`, counts only, no ids)
T1 verdicts by class (pilot items separately, excluded from accuracy claims). T2 `LANGUAGE_LIMITED` by class and by doctor. T3 accept rate by English-share band. T4 the same-week comparison: positives against N2 of the same ISO weeks (N1 descriptive only, v7.2 D1). T5 selection by the margin (A6): accepted against not accepted by doctor, flag class, route, tier and procedure category, and the accept rate per doctor. T6 the diagnostics: localized-versus-full own score (share falling by more than 0.15, by route), neighbour-in-padding, redaction counts beside every below-floor item, pipeline state and provenance counts, the SINGLE, INCOMPLETE and ambiguous-tie counts. T7 the procedure mix (mature, immature, scored). B stratum (technique changed) beside every A+ number. Nothing outside these tables is reported as a link-3 result.

## C2. The pin is symmetric and salted, for both readers (supersedes addendum 1 B's order rule)
- From the batch-3 read on, **each reader**, right after locking, runs `link3_nameleak.py pin` and posts **only the salted pin sha256(salt bytes ‖ his own counts-file bytes)**; neither the plain hash nor the salt goes on the bus; both salts go to the summary only. `summary` requires both pins and salts and recomputes each (`0e0f1ef`, eta-refuter-2 PASS #1573). Because a posted pin reveals nothing, **the order in which the readers post no longer matters**, so addendum 1 B's "both lock before either posts" rule is **superseded** (keeping it costs nothing and is allowed).
- `verify` (`link3_translate.py`, this commit) follows the summary's fields: a summary with `second_pin` must match `--second-pin`, carry well-formed `second_salt`, `counts_pin` and `counts_salt` (64 lower-case hex), and neither pin may be a reader's plain hash; summaries without `second_pin` (r2, b2) keep the plain comparison. v7.15a N2 step 2's "posts its sha256" means the **salted pin** from batch 3 on.

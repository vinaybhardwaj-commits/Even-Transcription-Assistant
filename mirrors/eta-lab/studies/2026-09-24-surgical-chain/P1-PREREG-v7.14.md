## S1. A harness fault is not silence and not a partial transcript (hist-miner #861, refuter #819)
hist-miner found that the research-pipeline harness dropped chunks it could decode (a duration-header test in `cut_window`), so some windows have **missing output** (`ok` false, `state` failed, `error = no_audio_in_window`, chunks dropped) or **partial audio**. The indexer now treats a record as finished only if `ok` is true, `state` is done, it has **no `error`** and **no dropped chunk**. Such a record is never indexed, so its item is never staged; "zero turns" is decided only on a clean, completed record (v7.10, v7.12 Q2). Where a window is re-transcribed under a new run, the **first clean output** is used. Checked now on the 81 windows indexed: none has an error or a dropped chunk. The four windows named (items c025 and c034) are failed runs and their items stay unstaged until re-transcribed. The same rule applies to the coverage pass; the August windows of the coverage list must come from the fixed harness.

## S2. How the coverage pass will be read, fixed BEFORE the new transcription lands (refuter #829)
The pass is a diagnostic reported **apart, labelled "outside the pre-registered window", with the offset from the Rx time**; it cannot change any item's primary verdict (the pre-registered window is plus or minus 10 minutes).
1. **`FOUND_ELSEWHERE`** for an item requires one sub-window (a room, a source, an offset) with all three of: own-prescription score at least 0.50; at least 0.15 above the best score of any other sub-window of that item; and at least 0.15 above the best same-day decoy prescription scored on that same sub-window. Anything else is `NOT_FOUND_IN_TEXT`. The decoy scoring is added to the pass when a sub-window first reaches 0.50 (none has so far).
2. **What each result means.** If at least 3 of the 8 items are `FOUND_ELSEWHERE` with offsets inside one 10-minute band, the reading is a **clock offset** between calendar and tape. If they are `FOUND_ELSEWHERE` at offsets within plus or minus 10 minutes but in rooms outside the shortlist, the reading is a **shortlist coverage** gap (rooms). If they are `FOUND_ELSEWHERE` at scattered offsets, it is per-item. **If none of the 8 is `FOUND_ELSEWHERE` once every room with tape in the span has been transcribed, the reading is that the visit is not in the recorded text, or is not matched by a complaint-and-diagnosis-only prescription: not a coverage problem.**
3. Production text and research-pipeline text stay apart; a result on one source is not confirmed by the other.

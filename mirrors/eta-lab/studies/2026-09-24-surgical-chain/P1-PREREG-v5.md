Replaces the rule in v4 (`P1-PREREG-v4.md`, edb2918). v4 was tried once on a 20-pair sample and **tripped its own threshold**: eta-refuter-2 (#605) graded 16 of 20 correct on the strict reading (80%; the threshold is 90%). Everything else in v1 to v4 stands. **No label is accepted and none has been made under any rule.** The rule is the code in `p1/procmatch.py`, committed with this file; stage F is run only after this commit.

## What the v4 sample showed, and what v5 changes
- **Two wrong matches (#5, #12).** #5: a vaginal hysterectomy advised, a vault re-exploration done; they shared only "vaginal". #12: a minor scar revision against a knee scar-tissue revision. v5: (a) when exactly one name is suffix-keyed (ends in -ectomy, -plasty and so on) the keyed root must appear in the other name, else `unknown`; (b) "scar", "tissue", "lesion", "mass", "growth", "operative", "assisted" join the generic STOP words; (c) vagina, vaginal, vault, perineal and pelvic join the site vocabulary; (d) laparoscopy and endoscopy are modality words, not procedures, and are ignored as keys.
- **Technique change, its own stratum (#6, #7).** Laser pilonidal ablation advised, excision with primary closure done: same indication, different technique. v5 returns `technique_changed` when two names share a content word (the same indication) but carry different action nouns (ablation and closure join the action-noun list). **Policy, decided now:** `technique_changed` is reported as its own stratum and **is never counted as a positive** until Fable or V rules on whether "proposed-and-done" means the same operation or surgery for the same indication (asked on the bus). `mismatch` and `unknown` are also their own strata. Refuter #605 counted endometriosis "operative laparoscopy" versus "radical excision" as a match; under v5 that pair stays `match` (only one side names an action noun).
- **Duplicates.** The two identical pairs (#6, #7) are two different patients who matched the same billing package name, not one patient with two prescriptions. **Label unit = (patient, advised procedure name):** one label per unit, keeping the earliest prescription. 28 of 130 candidate patients have two or more candidate prescriptions.

## A5. The positive label is per procedure, not per patient (refuter REQUIRED A)
- What the builder currently reports as "done-positives" (78 at 30 days, 119 at 14 days) is **advised Rx with an OT note or procedure billing line for the same patient within the window**. That is patient-level. It is a **candidate** set, not a label: a patient advised X and operated on for Y (an unrelated or second problem) is in it.
- **Label rule.** `proposed-and-done` = candidate AND `proc_match = match`. `mismatch` and `unknown` are reported as their own strata and are **never counted as positives**.
- **`proc_match` is computed on the patient side only** (advised name versus done-evidence name), with no transcript and no link score. The recording link never sees it, so gating the label with it is not circular (the circularity rule in v1 section 5 stands unchanged: `proc_match` stays out of links 1-3).
- **Rule, fixed now.** Names compared: advised = the structured array names (for `P_ONLY` with no structured name, the PSR `surgery_type` text, if any); done = OT-note `surgery_name`, procedure-billing item names, and `MANUAL` case `procedure_name` for the same patient in the window. Distinctive stems as in the builder's `stems()` (first 6 letters of a content word of 6 or more letters, generic surgical words removed). `match` if at least one distinctive stem is shared; `mismatch` if both sides have at least one distinctive stem and none is shared; `unknown` if either side has no name or no distinctive stem (this includes the 100 `UNNAMED` structured-flag Rx). A stage-2 adjudication of `mismatch` and `unknown` by the text judge is allowed and is reported as a separate column; eta-refuter-2 checks precision on 20 pairs. Stage 1 alone defines the label until stage 2 is accepted.
- The report says "candidate" for the patient-level set and "labelled positive" only for `match`.

## A6. Selection by the margin (refuter REQUIRED B)
A surgeon's same-day patients often share a complaint, so a margin of 0.15 will tend to pass unusual presentations and drop typical ones. Accepted chains may be a biased subset. Pre-registered check, produced with every accept-rate table:
- For positives and for N1 separately: **accepted versus not accepted, split by doctor, specialty, flag class, route, tier and procedure category** (`ptype` key), and the **accept rate per doctor**, with the n in every cell (no suppression, no silent pooling).
- **Any label-rate claim is made on the accepted set with those tables beside it.** A rate quoted without them is not a P1 result.
- **Residual circularity through the complaint** (refuter note): "hernia" in the complaint predicts hernia-repair talk. For accepted chains, report the P2 "operation discussed" rate against the `link_score` tertile. A strong rise across tertiles is read as selection on the topic and reported as such.

## A7. Plain CT: done source and its limits (ruling 164; mb-rx #571, `scout-rx.md` section 7)
- **Scope.** Plain CT = a CT order whose name is not angiography, calcium score, MRI or PET; matched on `investigation__uid` where possible. MRI, X-ray, echo, treadmill and calcium score stay out. CT angiography stays advised-only and referred out (rulings 150, 163).
- **`offered_in_house = true`** for plain CT (V, ruling 163).
- **Done source `RAD`** for the same patient within [Rx `timestamp` - 1 day, + 30 days]: (a) a `chart_service_request` with status COMPLETED and CT modality (not angio or calcium), time `occurrence_period__start`; (b) a `kx_billing_records` plain-CT line (uhid to individual; inpatient and emergency only: only 7 outpatient radiology lines exist). **Weaker class `RAD_IN_PROGRESS`:** REQUESTED, BOOKED, PENDING_CHECKIN. **Never done:** PRESCRIBED or DRAFT (a lead created with the Rx, as for `PRESCRIBED` cases). Match by patient plus time, not by Rx uid (`prescription_uid` is filled on only 0-50% of these rows).
- **Plain CT is positives-only, never negatives, until the record is complete.** mb-rx measured that the platform table holds roughly a third of in-house CTs (HIS shows 14-22 plain CTs a week in Jun-Jul against 2-7 in the platform table), that the complete table (`kx_radiology_reports`) stops on 13 Jul, and that **0 of the 32 plain-CT Rx dated on or before 24 Aug have a done record** (all 20 hits are from 25 Aug on). So absence of a CT record is not evidence of "not done", and no plain-CT Rx enters N1 or N2. Plain-CT chains that do have a done record are candidate positives under A5 (procedure gate: the ordered CT versus the done CT's name and body part).
- **Maturity for plain CT:** the same cutoffs (22 Aug, 30 days; 7 Sep, 14 days). On present data mature plain-CT positives are close to zero; this is reported, not worked around.

## A8. Notes carried into every report (refuter #559)
- **N1 is named "no evidence of done in our records"**, not "not done": a procedure done elsewhere, or a same-day OPD procedure, is invisible to OT notes and inpatient billing.

That premise changed (v2 A1: the key now resolves for 99.7% of visits across the window). The report still gives the n per route cell (`KEY`, `HEUR`, `RXWIN`) and never pools silently.

## A1.1 The mechanism, and the one deviation from the ruling's wording
Ruling 261 names "model re-queried person-only, minus day/month/drug/clinical/brand allowlists". surg-deid measured that method on 418 candidate token occurrences (bus #1381): the disputed token's person score is 0.06 and 300 of 418 occurrences score exactly 0; a threshold chosen without reference to it (0.3) does not catch it and only a threshold of 0.05 or lower does, which would be fitted to the one known token (v7.15b R3.1 forbids that; eta-refuter-2 withdrew the suggestion, #1387). The **blanket rule** below is used instead: the only candidate whose scope the token did not set. It is a different mechanism from the ruling's text with the same aim; ruling 273 confirms it: no unfitted threshold catches the disputed token (person score 0.06) and a fitted 0.05 is refused.

## A1.2 The rule (as the committed code, flag `--cap-rule` of `deid.py`; off by default, default output byte-identical to main)
After all other layers, a **Title-case word of 3 or more letters in mid-sentence** (the previous non-space character is not `.`, `!` or `?`, and it is not inside any `[...]` span) that **no layer tagged** is tagged `[PATIENT_NAME]`, unless it is on an allowlist **fixed in advance**: full day and month names (and plural days; abbreviations such as Jan, Sun, Sat, Sept are **not** allowlisted, because they are also names); word stems of 5 or more letters of the procedure lexicon (`lexicon.py`); the existing never-a-name set (pronouns, role, function and nationality words). Roster doctors and the consult's own doctor are kept, also when the model kept only part of a capitalised run. ALL-CAPS words, sentence-initial words and lowercase words are not covered (they remain the model's job). Adjacent tagged words become one tag. Plus the **"-in-law" edge-trim fix** (always on; it removes false positives only). No token from the 119 turns or from any set is in the code. There is **no drug, clinical-term or brand lexicon on the box** (`lexicon.py` is procedures only), so drug names, clinical terms and brands are tagged and counted; ruling 261 accepts places and brands as over-redaction. **No generic-drug stem list is added (ruling 273: NO for v7.15b; freeze now).** Drug over-redaction is accepted for this stratum; the linker MEASURES drug tokens tagged in the 19-item run and may pre-register a stem list in v7.16 before that run.

## A1.5 Disclosures
- The rule was designed after the gate failed on one turn; its scope was not set by that token (surg-deid's measurement of the alternative is why); R3 of v7.15b, written before any regenerated text existed, governs it.
- The blanket rule differs from the mechanism named in ruling 261; the ruling on that difference is ruling 273 (YES, blanket rule; supersedes 261's wording).
- The rule tags places, brands, clinical terms and (on the box) drug names; the classes and counts are above.
- **Known leak class of the allowlist (eta-refuter-2 F2):** the abbreviations were removed from the allowlist by surg-deid after review (they are also names); what remains is a small fixed class: **a person named exactly May, June, April, August or March is not tagged by this rule** (the model layer still applies to them). No adjacent-date-or-number exemption was added (it would be a second rule authored after the failure); the full read under R2 still counts any such name as a miss.
- **The "-in-law" trim is always on (eta-refuter-2 F3):** default output (no `--cap-rule`) is **byte-identical to main except where a model span ends in "in-law" or "in law", on every default caller including the surgery path**, where it un-redacts the relation word ("daughter-[PATIENT_NAME]" becomes "daughter-in-law"). It only removes false positives; it changes 0 of the 17,848 turns and 1 of the 119.
- **Bracket artefacts (eta-refuter-2 F1):** fixed before the freeze: a token inside any `[...]` span is exempt ("[Inaudible Speech]" is left alone; text after the bracket is still covered), with a test.
- A change to any rule above is a new dated version.

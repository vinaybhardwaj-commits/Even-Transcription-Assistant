# SURGICAL-CHAIN-LINKER P1: pre-registration v7.6 (linker, 24 Sep 2026): the link text and question for real pairs (found while preparing the ruling-191 pilot)

Addendum to v7.5; nothing earlier is edited. **No real pair scored; no patient text sent anywhere.** Code: `p1/link3_judge.py` (`LINK_QUESTION_V2`, `rx_link_text`, `v2_check`), committed with this file; the synthetic run happens afterwards. Disclosure: I found the two problems below by counting fields on the 4 pilot prescriptions and the size of their room windows (structure and counts only), before any text was fetched, de-identified or sent.

## J1. Two problems with the frozen v7.3 question, and the fixes
1. **Surgical-clinic prescriptions often list no medicines.** Of the 4 pilot prescriptions, 3 have no medicine rows in either the hub or the pipeline copy. The frozen question asks that the medicines be consistent, which is undefined for them. **Fix:** the question judges the complaint and the diagnosis, and the medicines only when the prescription lists any.
2. **A room window is several visits.** The pre-registered window (consult plus or minus 10 minutes) holds 65 to 706 turns (2,700 to 15,800 characters) per shortlisted room in the pilot items: several patients' visits. "Do they describe the same visit" is ill-posed for that. **Fix:** the question asks whether **any one visit** in the transcript is the one the prescription was written for. The window stays plus or minus 10 minutes (ruling 191).

## J2. The link text (fixed) and the question (frozen, verbatim in code as `LINK_QUESTION_V2`)
- **Prescription text = symptoms + diagnosis names + medicines (or "none listed").** The plan of management and each diagnosis's advice and treatment-plan text are **excluded**: that is where an advised operation is named (v1 section 5, `proc_used_in_link = false`). The offline self-test asserts that such text never reaches the link text.
- **Route unchanged:** local de-identification of both sides (`deid.py` on the GPU, then the residual-identifier gate that fails closed on Indic script or an untranslated turn) before any Jev call; redaction counts per side per item; the pilot rooms are all English.
- **Synthetic acceptance for the new question (16 + 24 invented calls), fixed now:** M1 (three-visit window that contains the prescription's visit, with medicines): p(yes) >= 0.70 for at least 3 of 4. M2 (three-visit window that does not): p(yes) <= 0.30 for at least 3 of 4. M3 (no-medicine prescriptions, single visits; two match, two do not): correct (match >= 0.70, non-match <= 0.30) for at least 3 of 4. **And the v7.3a hard set re-run under the new question:** H1 and H2 own >= 0.50 and margin >= 0.15 for at least 3 of 4 in each group; H3 descriptive. If any fails, the wording is revised in a v7.7 from synthetic pairs only.
- Floor 0.50, margin 0.15, the room and same-day-prescription margins, the SINGLE and INCOMPLETE classes, the same-pipeline rule and the pilot's status (a mechanics check, not a result) are unchanged.

## J3. Consequence to expect (a note, not a rule)
Prescriptions with no medicines are judged on complaint and diagnosis alone, so their same-day neighbours with the same diagnosis will tie more often than the medicine-bearing ones in the hard set; the 23% upper bound (identical diagnosis-code sets) was computed on diagnosis alone and stands as the estimate.

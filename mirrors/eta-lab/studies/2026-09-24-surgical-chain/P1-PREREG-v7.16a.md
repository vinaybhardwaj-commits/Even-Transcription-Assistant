## F3. Line isolation, in `mt-b3/` only (the rule change Fable approved; it changes v7.15 M2's "never partly used" from item level to line level for `mt-b3/` only)
1. A batch of up to 25 lines is sent and repeated exactly as in M2. **If the batch has used its permitted repeat and is still not accepted, its lines are re-sent ONE LINE PER CALL**, each call under the same M2 rules (429/5xx backoff; any other failure or malformed reply is repeated once at the same settings; a second failure ends that line).
2. A line that fails twice is **isolated-failed**: it gets a record with `q = "mt_failed"` and **`en = ""` (no text kept, none repaired by hand, none machine-guessed)**, keeping its `item`, `room`, `i`, `lang` and `at`. The screen (`link3_screen.py`, unchanged) drops any record whose flag is not `ok`, so an isolated-failed line is **dropped and counted** (it appears under the screen's existing "unflagged" reason, since its flag is not one of the three); it never reaches the name-leak draw (eligible = `q == "ok"`) or Jev.
3. **The report shows, per item, how many lines were isolated-failed** (from the provenance `isolated_failed_lines` count and the log's per-line reasons; counts only).
4. **An item is `MT_FAILED` (final) only if** a route check fails (model other than requested, provider not reported or changed within the item, a non-retryable HTTP failure that repeats) **or every one of its lines is isolated-failed**. Otherwise it is `MT_OK` with its isolated-failed lines counted; its kept turns are the ones Gemini flagged `ok`.
5. All other v7.15 to v7.16 rules apply unchanged: **cap rule `a53717d`** de-identification (no stem list), the **full read** (every eligible turn, blind, under v7.15b R2, refuter-2 pins first, any total above zero fails, then fix and full re-read; minimum 20 eligible: **fewer refuses**, is reported and not lowered), the C screen, `verify` (with the batch item check), scoring (floor 0.50, margin 0.15, `LINK_QUESTION_V2`), the residual-identifier gate, hash bindings and the pin.

## T1. Where the translated stratum's results come from (fixed now)
- **Item set = all 19 LANGUAGE_LIMITED items of the final main run**, each with **one final translated-stratum record**: batch 1 (`mt/`, 8 items), batch 2 (`mt-b2/`, the 7 items that translated on the first attempt) and batch 3 (`mt-b3/`, the 4 items of the one fresh attempt, v7.16a). **Merge rule:** an item's final record is the batch-3 record if the item is in batch 3 (its batch-2 record was `MT_FAILED` and is kept as attempt 1), else its own batch's record. Files read: `translated_results.json` of each directory, `provenance.json`, and the batch-1 `translated_results_addendum.json` (c026's reason) and batch-2 addendum (c021's exclusion) for the fields the older results did not carry.
- **No text is read**: verdicts, scores, margins, counts and statuses only.

## T3. The tables (all counts; doctors are not shown; no item ids in the output)
- **T8a, verdicts by class, all 19 items, reported apart:** for each class (`POS <stratum>`, `N1`, `N2`) the counts of final translated verdicts (`ACCEPTED`, `AMBIGUOUS_TIE`, `REJECT_BELOW_FLOOR`, `SINGLE`, `INCOMPLETE`, `MT_FAILED`), with **the excluded items shown as a separate row** ("excluded, not counted"). Never merged into T1 and never a denominator of T1 to T7.
- **T8b, the yield (the only translated-stratum accuracy statement): non-excluded items only:** per class, the number of items scored (`ACCEPTED`, `AMBIGUOUS_TIE`, `REJECT_BELOW_FLOOR`, `SINGLE`) and the number `ACCEPTED`, with the count of non-excluded items `INCOMPLETE` or `MT_FAILED` beside it (not scored). **No rate is computed if the scored count is 0.** The expected number of non-excluded scored items is at most 13 (19 minus 6 excluded); a rate on so few items is descriptive only and is stated with its counts.
- **T8c, how the translation went (from provenance and the screen reports, counts only):** per batch and total: items `MT_OK` / `MT_FAILED` (attempt 1 and final), turns in, kept (Gemini `ok`), dropped by reason (`garbled`, `untranslatable`, `unflagged`), **isolated-failed lines** (v7.16a), retries and repeats, and the structural malformed-reply reasons where logged.
- **T8d, what translation changed, item by item as counts:** the cross-table of the **English-only flagged verdict** (v7.12) by the **final translated verdict**, over all 19 items, with the excluded items marked. It shows, without a rate, whether translation ever moved a verdict; **for an accepted item it also states whether the best-scoring room was a translated room or a native English room** (from the stored `best_room` against the selected translated rooms), so that an accept that does not depend on translation is visible as such.

## T4. What is not done
No pooling of the translated stratum with any native stratum in any table, count or claim; no re-scoring; no change of any earlier table; no new selection. If any of the 19 items ends `MT_FAILED` or `INCOMPLETE` it is counted as such, not dropped.

## R3. Which lines are kept (fixed now)
- **English turns (`lang` en) are kept as they are** (native whisper English; no translation call is made for them).
- **An Indic turn is kept iff** its 337b item exists in the snapshot, has `en` and no `err`, has `q == "ok"`, and passes the `src_sha256` check. **Dropped, and counted by reason:** `garbled`, `untranslatable` (the translator's own flags: never re-translated, never guessed), `mt_failed` (failure items), `missing_337b` (no item for that segment in the snapshot), `stale`, `empty_en`. A dropped line does not appear in any text sent anywhere.
- **UNCOVERED:** a **window** is UNCOVERED if it has Indic turns and **none** of them is kept (in particular a window with no 337b file at all); an **item** is UNCOVERED if **none of its selected rooms has a kept Indic turn** (a T9 result needs at least one kept 337b English line; kept English turns alone are the native stratum's business, not T9's). UNCOVERED items are **reported and not scored, not forced**. All other rules of an item with some kept turns are as before (an item whose best room has no kept turn inside the consult ±2 min is INCOMPLETE, unchanged).

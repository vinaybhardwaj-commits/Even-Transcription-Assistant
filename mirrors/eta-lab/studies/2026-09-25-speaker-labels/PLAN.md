## Addendum 1 (25 Sep ~08:55): PILOT findings and a rule change made BEFORE the full reference run and before any label is shown
Facts found on the reference set (317 windows = the 318 of ruling 350 minus one that fails the >= 90 % coverage test; 11 rooms; 4,744 segments; 79.2 audio-h): every one is a 15-min hist-bulk window with EXACTLY the span of its production window (start/end offset 0 s for all 317), so the pyannote.ai turns need no clock offset; only 11 have recording gaps > 1 s (the gap-closed mapping matters for those; implemented and tested in `speaker_labels.closed_to_wall`).
Pilot on 12 of them (Nemotron on the box T4, whole 15-min windows, ~11.5 s per window): most transcript segments are FIXED ~30 s BLOCKS (median 29.7 s; 94 of 118 >= 20 s), containing pauses and often several speakers, and no word times exist. So the first rule ("unlabeled if < 30 % of the segment duration is covered") wrongly rejected real speech (45 % of long segments). CHANGED to: unlabeled iff < 1.0 s of diarized speech (any speaker) in the segment; otherwise the dominant speaker by overlap seconds, `purity`, a `mixed` flag (second speaker >= 30 % of the segment's diarized speech and >= 2 s) and `sequence` = the speaker letters in time order inside the segment ('A > B > A'), because one label per 30 s block would be false where speakers alternate. Unit tests updated (18/18). Pre-registered measures 1-4 stand unchanged; only the definition of 'unlabeled/mixed' changed.
Pilot numbers (12 windows, 9 scorable, segment-level agreement of Nemotron vs pyannote.ai after the best relabelling, weighted by segment duration): median 0.985, mean 0.895, p10 0.688, segment-weighted 0.863; speaker count equal in 4 of 12 (mean abs diff 1.33). Unlabeled share differs: pyannote.ai 59 % of segments vs Nemotron 34 %: pyannote.ai hears no speech (< 1 s) in more of the transcribed blocks than Nemotron does, consistent with Nemotron's known false-alarm-heavy behaviour (V5 FA 0.27 vs teacher) and with many of these 30 s blocks being near-silence text (see the refused-window results). Interpretation limit: no ground truth for who spoke; agreement between two engines where both hear speech is evidence of a stable partition, not of correctness. Full reference-set run in progress (317 windows); results to `RESULTS-REFERENCE.md`.

# 337e reference-set validation: Nemotron-3 (option B) vs the pyannote.ai-labelled windows (ruling 350) — diar-lab, 25 Sep
Set: 317 vault windows (Fable's 318 minus one failing the >= 90 % coverage test), all 15-min hist-bulk windows with EXACTLY the span of a production window carrying a pyannote.ai label (start/end offset 0 s for all); 11 rooms, 18-24 Sep. 315 had transcript segments to score (2 had none). Nemotron-3-Diarization base, offline 30.4 s, run on the box T4 (317 windows, 2,692 s); pyannote.ai turns mapped closed -> wall clock through each window's chunk plan (`speaker_labels.closed_to_wall`, 18 tests). Labels per transcript segment by `speaker_labels.assign` (unlabeled iff < 1.0 s of diarized speech; dominant speaker by overlap seconds; mixed iff a second speaker holds >= 30 % and >= 2 s; rule fixed after the pilot, PLAN addendum 1). Numbers only; `ref_scores.json` (per window).
## Result (pre-registered measures 1-3 of PLAN.md)
| measure | value |
|---|---|
| windows scored (>= 1 segment labelled by BOTH engines) | 297 of 315 |
| segment-level speaker agreement after the best relabelling, per window | **median 0.989**, mean 0.908, p10 0.667; 243 of 297 windows >= 0.8; 17 < 0.6 |
| duration-weighted over all jointly labelled segments | **0.927** |
| speaker count per window equal (Nemotron = pyannote.ai) | 118 of 315 (37 %); mean abs difference 0.98 |
| segments unlabeled (< 1 s diarized speech) | pyannote.ai 26.5 %, Nemotron 16.0 % |
| segments 'mixed' (a second speaker >= 30 % and >= 2 s) | pyannote.ai 22.4 %, Nemotron 24.8 % |
1. **Time-mapping check (measure 1), 10 windows with recording gaps:** agreement with the gap mapping median 0.901 (mean 0.874) vs without (closed axis read as wall) 0.895 (0.872); identical to 2 decimals in 8 of 10 windows. The gaps in this set are small, so the mapping is neutral here (it does not hurt; it matters only for large gaps). The 287 gap-free windows are identical with or without it. For the Nemotron labels themselves no mapping is needed (they are computed on the wall-clock audio the transcript used).
2. **Purity (measure 2):** about 75-78 % of labelled segments are single-speaker (not 'mixed') for both engines; ~1 in 4 blocks contains a second speaker (transcript segments are mostly fixed ~30 s blocks, so a block often holds turns of two people; that is why the label is the dominant speaker plus the order of speakers inside the block).
3. **Agreement (measure 3):** where both engines hear speech in a segment they give the same partition of speakers in the large majority of segment time (0.927 duration-weighted, median window 0.989). The 17 poor windows are mostly low-evidence: mean 5.8 jointly labelled segments (vs 14.1 in good windows), 8 of 17 have <= 3 such segments, and Nemotron finds more speakers there (3.6 vs pyannote.ai 2.8).
## Reading and limits
- Agreement between two engines is evidence of a STABLE partition, not of correctness: there is no human ground truth for who spoke. Both are automatic; earlier work showed Nemotron under-counts minor speakers and marks more speech than pyannote.ai (false alarm), which appears here as fewer unlabeled segments (16 % vs 26.5 %) and different speaker counts (equal in 37 % of windows).
- The labels are for the DOMINANT speaker of a segment and its speaker sequence, not a per-word attribution; letters (A, B, C by first appearance) are stable only inside a window, not across windows.
- Doctor/patient names are out of scope here (later, from the voice-check work).
## Pipeline decision (ruling 364: run on eta-lab-l4)
Audio for the full index (3,794 windows, 969 audio-h) is cut on the box and shipped to the L4 in batches. An Opus 32 kbps re-encode was tried first (13.6 MB per audio-h) and REJECTED: on 20 windows the wav-vs-opus Nemotron labels agreed only median 0.995 / mean 0.919 (min 0.649), speaker count equal in 18 of 20, 6 of 274 segments changed labelled/unlabeled. FLAC is LOSSLESS (verified bit-exact round trip, 50 % of wav, ~56 GB for the index, ~23 min of transfer at the measured 41 MB/s, hidden by pipelining), so the results equal the wav results. Scripts: `cut_batch.py` (full-length windows, FLAC), `l4_consume.py` (decode + Nemotron under flock, per batch), `full_run.py` (orchestrator). Audio is deleted after each batch; only turns (no text, no audio) are kept.

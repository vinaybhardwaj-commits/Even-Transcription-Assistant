## D1. Data (counts as of 26 Sep 10:35 box clock)
- **Consults:** the PQM consult steps of 25 Sep from mb-rx's `encounter-truth/rows.csv` (192 rows; 189 completed; 34 doctors); each carries its Rx uid(s) by doctor and time (the truth table's own join, rx_link = doctor+time for all 192). Consults are opaque token uids; no patient identity is pulled.
- **Prescriptions:** `dpipe_prescription_pipeline` (197 non-draft Rx for those consults) and its child tables `__medicines`, `__diagnosis`, `__further_investigation`, `__referral`, `__plan_of_management`; drug brand and generic names from the Pulse `medicines` master by the medicine uid. Read-only Metabase (the read key file, never printed).
- **Tape:** hist-bulk tier-C windows of 25 Sep (395 windows, 10 rooms, about 116,700 words), the first ok record per window; segments carry absolute times (window start plus start_s), language, engine, refused (+reason). **English = the 337b annotation (gemini-3.8-flash) where it exists; `transcript_english` is null in every record.** Where 337b has not run, an Indic line has NO English (recorded as null, not guessed).

## D2. EXTRACT (`cap_extract.py`)
Items per Rx, with a stable id `sha1(consult|class|normalised text)[:12]`: **drug** (name; brand and generic alternatives from the master), **dose** (name + strength, dosage, frequency, duration), **diagnosis** (diagnosis rows and the plan's diagnosis-or-impression, with ICD), **investigation** (further_investigation names), **advice** (the plan's general advice, treatment plan and management plan text, split into sentence-level atoms), **referral** (specialist type), **followup** (type and date). 1,385 items over 178 consults at freeze.

## D3. LINK (`cap_link.py`): a consult is tied to a room span, never a room to a doctor
- **Span** = called_at minus 30 s to completed_at plus 30 s. **Candidate rooms** = rooms with at least 20 s of speech inside the span (almost all consults have 4 to 8 such rooms, so time alone does not decide).
- **Evidence terms:** complaint = words of the Rx `presenting_complaint` (a field that is NOT a scored item, so it is non-circular); D = words of the diagnosis items; M = words of the drug names (brand and generic). A term counts as present in a room span if it occurs (or fuzzy-matches at ratio 0.82) in the room's English text, the 337b English of its Indic lines, or its Latin-script words. Weights are idf over the day's room windows.
- **Score** = matched idf / total idf of the level's terms (0 to 1). **Levels (basis):** `TC` = complaint; `TCD` = complaint + diagnosis; `TCDM` = complaint + diagnosis + drugs. **Status:** confirmed if the best room's score is at or above the null's 99th percentile, at least 2 terms matched and the margin over the second room is at least 0.15; probable if at or above the null's 90th percentile, at least 1 term matched, best strictly above second; else none. **The null** = the same term sets scored against spans of the same length 90 to 150 minutes away (four shifts), computed before any capture class exists (`link_thresholds.json`). The final link is the highest-status level, ties to the LEAST circular level; **the basis is recorded on every link**.
- **used_for_link:** true for diagnosis items on TCD and TCDM links and for drug items on TCDM links. Class rates are reported separately for links without drug matching (basis TC, TCD), for TCDM, and in and out of `used_for_link` (eta-refuter-2 #3461).
- **Tried and rejected (disclosed):** adding the same Rx's non-scored history and examination text (hub table fields `presenting_complaints` and `examination`) to the complaint terms was run once on 26 Sep, after the first link counts (chosen because the link rate was low, not because of any capture class, none existed): it gave fewer confirmed links (8 versus 26) because more terms dilute the matched fraction, so it is OFF (`USE_HUB_TEXT = False`); the published bundle v1 is the complaint-only run.
- **Not done:** no room-to-doctor binding; no doctor voiceprint (none exists for 25 Sep's windows on the box); no Jev.
- **Known limit:** many consults may be in rooms that were not taped, and a 'none' cannot tell an untaped consult from a failed link; the link rate per room is reported as measured, not as an error rate.

## D5. The ceiling check (step 4; run from V's terminal, script reviewed by eta-refuter-2 first)
1. cap posts the sha of the full **ABSENT** item-id list (ABSENT only, not ABSENT-SPEECH-GAP). linker then commits `sha256(salt || that sha)` (the salt stays private) and draws **60 ABSENT items at random** with `random.Random(seed_material)` over the sorted ids; eta-refuter-2 redraws from the revealed salt.
2. **Decoys:** for each consult in the batch, real drug, diagnosis, investigation and advice terms that are NOT on that consult's prescription, matched to the target's class and to its frequency bucket over the day's items; the same prompt; the question order shuffled; **Gemini is blind to which is which**. The false-yes rate on decoys is reported beside the yes rate on ABSENT targets.
3. The prompt to Gemini gives the consult's audio (rebuilt from `cut.chunks_used` with lab-mover's frozen harness, read-only import) and a list of terms, and asks yes / no / unclear with a timestamp for each term ("said aloud, allowing brand or generic names and translation"). **Scoring-only:** the outputs go to the CAP study dir only and never to anywhere a transcript, prompt, glossary or correction reads. PHI to Gemini is allowed by V's standing rule (Vertex, or OpenRouter with the ZDR provider); the pane guard blocks external sends of patient data from Claude panes, so the run is by V from a terminal, like 337b.
4. Both rates are reported: "said aloud but lost" (yes on ABSENT targets, corrected by the decoy false-yes rate) and "never said" (no).

## D6. CAP-2 (design only, not built; after Fable's ruling on CAP-1)
- **Vocabulary source:** Pulse prescription history **excluding 25 and 26 Sep** (so no same-day leakage): per doctor and per specialty, the drugs (brand and generic, with strengths), diagnoses, investigations and advice phrases with counts. The specialty of a room comes from the bench name in the R2 key (for example `opd-4-ortho`), not from a doctor binding.
- **Levers:** (1) the **337b translation glossary** (text only, a Gemini prompt: a short list of known terms for the room's specialty, "prefer these spellings when a word is close"); this is the only lever the pinned stack allows. (2) **STT prompting** (whisper `prompt` field) only through a direct whisper call as a labelled ARM run: the production shim and router do not pass a prompt and IndicConformer has no hotword mechanism (hist-bulk's facts).
- **Evaluation:** re-run a stratified 25 Sep sample (by room, language mix and doctor) with three repeats of the glossary arm AND three repeats of a no-glossary control arm through the same path, because a re-run of the same window differs by about 0.4 in similarity (temperature fallback): the steering effect is the between-arm difference against the within-arm spread. Compare the CAP-1 class rates on the SAME items.
- **Risks to measure:** hotword hallucination (drug terms inserted where nothing was said): the decoy method of D5 gives a false-insertion rate; nothing from the 25 Sep prescriptions may enter the vocabulary, the glossary or the prompt; evaluation items are scored only after the run.

## D7. Changes after eta-refuter-2's review of bundle v1 (26 Sep 11:00 box clock; #3491, #3474, #3506)
- **Link calibration (#3491.1):** the thresholds are now calibrated through `decide()` itself (best room over all candidates, the margin rules, the best level), on the wrong-time control (spans shifted by -150, -90, +90 and +150 minutes; 667 null trials): the smallest percentile pair with a false-link rate of at most 5% (probable + confirmed) and at most 1% (confirmed) is used; the control result and the EXPECTED false links (any 7.6, confirmed 1.6 over 182 consults with a candidate room) are written to `link_thresholds.json`. Bundle v1's single-room percentiles are withdrawn.
- **One row per consult token (#3491.2):** 183 tokens from 189 rows; the span of a two-step token is the earliest called_at to the latest completed_at, the Rx the union.
- **used_for_link (#3491.3):** any item, of any class, whose words hit the term set that MADE its consult's link (complaint, patient-name, diagnosis, drug words).
- **time_src (#3491.4, #3474.2):** `pqm` (queue_token_steps CONSULTATION called_at/completed_at through mb-rx's encounter-truth rows) on every link.
- **New non-scored evidence in TC:** the patient's name words from the Rx `name` field beside the complaint words (PHI: private raw dir, never in the bundle); chosen on the wrong-time control only (27 links versus 21), before any capture class exists. The summed-idf metric gave 20 links; the history and examination text stays OFF.
- **Headline non-circular rates** are links at basis TC only (#3474.1); TCD and TCDM links carry used_for_link items reported in and out.
- **translation_missing (#3474.4):** every link records `n_indic_segments` and `n_indic_without_english`; cap keeps translation_missing consults out of CAPTURED versus LOST-IN-TRANSLATION until 337b's English exists for 25 Sep.
- **Ceiling (#3506):** the ABSENT-list sha is the sha256 of the sorted JSON list of unique ids and `draw` refuses any other list; targets come only from CONFIRMED links; the bundle MANIFEST sha is recorded in draw.json and `run` and `score` refuse if it changed; `cut_window.py` (pin 0fb0eed981de2a48) and `full_audio.py` (pin cf06ca69f8075e60) are hash-checked before import; the result reports targets drawn versus run, unparsed and missing counts, and states that the decoy yes rate is an upper bound on the false-yes rate.

CONF_THRESHOLD = 0.70

DEFINITIONS
D1  DOUBTFUL / CONFIDENT (ruling 186): CONFIDENT requires margin >= 0.10 AND best >= 0.50, where best = the turn's cosine to the verified centroid of the room-day's confirmed doctor and margin = best minus the best other cluster or centroid in the window; only verified centroids (ruling 182 controls) count. Everything else is DOUBTFUL, as are turns with speaker_basis = diarization_only or Nemotron and pyannote counts that disagree. A missing best or margin = DOUBTFUL. The arm 1 export must carry both `best` and `margin` (null allowed, keys required). Older text for the inputs: (runner-up per ruling 167: best other cluster in the window, else the best other stored doctor centroid for the room, else margin missing and DOUBTFUL): margin in ECAPA space against the stored doctor centroids where they exist (166 A), the 165 C profile built with ECAPA otherwise, diarizer counts recomputed fresh (165 C). Missing margin (no embeddable audio, no doctor profile) = DOUBTFUL. A missing diarizer count is NOT a disagreement. This scorer takes the flag from the arm 1 file and cross-checks it against the frozen rule.
D2  Arm 1 answer = production's speaker role (addendum D): role "clinician" = DOCTOR; an empty role or any other role = NOT DOCTOR. The count of arm-1 not-doctor turns that had an empty role is reported separately.
D3  Q1 to is_doctor: "doctor" is doctor; "patient", "companion", "staff" are not doctor; "cannot_tell" is UNSURE.
D4  Jev confidence = the `confidence` field of the Q1 answer (calibrated confidence of the chosen option), NOT probabilities[choice].
D5  Truth (166 B, priority order): (1) V's existing labels (split-speaker pilot answers, V-verified listening clips, Salanki verified turns), mapped to turns by time overlap >= 50%, and the 70 recovered E-3 Cardiology turns (ruling 185); (3) the "Who is speaking" page, only as a top-up (size = the shortfall to >= 60 labelled doubtful and >= 30 labelled confident turns, ruling 188). A turn labelled by several sources uses only its highest-priority source that has a usable answer (v_existing, e3_recovered, vpilot_voice_verified, who_is_speaking, in that order). Truth is binary doctor vs not-doctor. "Cannot tell" answers are ignored. Scored only on turns with at least 1 usable answer. If the usable answers disagree on doctor vs not-doctor the turn is excluded and reported (counts of both exclusions are in results.json).

ONE RULE FOR ARMS 2, 3 AND 4 (addendum E)
R1  CONFIDENT turn: final = arm 1.
R2  DOUBTFUL turn, arms 2 and 3: if Q1 choice is not "cannot_tell" and Q1 confidence >= CONF_THRESHOLD, final = Q1 choice mapped by D3. Otherwise UNSURE.
R3  DOUBTFUL turn, arm 4: L1 "marked_middle_speaker" = doctor; "not_marked_middle_speaker" = not doctor; "cannot_tell" or a failed call = UNSURE. No threshold (Gemini gives no calibrated confidence).
R4  UNSURE, cannot tell, or a failed or missing call = WRONG for accuracy. The counts are reported separately (unsure_n, unsure_doubtful_n, errors_n).
R5  Q2 and Q3 are recorded. They do not enter the combine rule. Q3 is scored as a secondary count metric (S6).
R6  Confident sample (ruling 169, widened by 188): arms 2-4 are also run on every CONFIDENT turn that carries usable human truth, from any source (V's pilot labels, the recovered E-3 turns, the "Who is speaking" page's confident items). vj-data supplies their ids (ids only; label values stay in the truth store). The arm's DIRECT answer there (no combine) is compared with arm 1 and with truth.

SCORING DEFINITIONS
S1  Accuracy = correct / n, separately on DOUBTFUL and CONFIDENT turns that have usable truth, per arm. Correct = final is_doctor equals truth is_doctor.
S2  Paired comparison with arm 1 on DOUBTFUL turns: b = arm right and arm 1 wrong, c = arm wrong and arm 1 right. gain_pp = 100 x (arm accuracy - arm 1 accuracy), UNWEIGHTED. p = exact McNemar (two-sided exact binomial on b + c discordant pairs), UNWEIGHTED, on the paired sample. Population weighting (ruling 215): gain_pp_weighted = 100 x (weighted arm accuracy - weighted arm 1 accuracy), where the weighted accuracy is the per-role accuracy on the labelled DOUBTFUL turns combined with weights = production role shares among ALL DOUBTFUL turns in the arm 1 file (role null or empty = "empty"; roles with no labelled turn are dropped and the weights renormalised, and are listed). The weights are the PINNED shares in data/role_shares_doubtful.json key all_pool_doubtful (distinct production turns; vj-refuter #1067), passed with --role-weights and stamped with the file's sha in results.json; shares recomputed from the arm 1 file are not pinned (arm1_in has per-context turn ids) and are flagged as such.
S3  Confident-sample flips = confident-sample turns (with usable human truth) where arm 1 was right and the arm's direct answer is wrong or UNSURE. Also reported: would_have_changed (direct answer differs from arm 1 and is not UNSURE), unsure, direct accuracy vs arm 1 accuracy on the same turns.
S4  PASS BAR (addendum F), computed as meets_pass_bar, all five needed:
    F1 at least 60 DOUBTFUL turns with usable truth;
    F2 gain_pp_weighted >= 5 AND the unweighted gain_pp has the same sign (is > 0);
    F3 exact McNemar p < 0.05 and b > c, unweighted (ruling 215);
    F4 (ruling 169) at least 30 confident human-labelled turns run directly, and zero flips (S3). With fewer than 30, F4 = INSUFFICIENT: the arm cannot be adopted for production, only continued (status CONTINUE_ONLY when F1-F3 and F5 hold);
    F5 stability (S5) holds.
    meets_pass_bar (status ADOPTABLE / CONTINUE_ONLY / NOT_ADOPTED) is a computed flag, not a verdict. The verdict is Fable's and the refuter's.
S5  Stability (addendum G): the refuter re-queries the arm at temperature 0 on a random 20% of the scored DOUBTFUL turns (the re-query file may not cover less than floor(20% x n_doubtful)). Holds = raw-answer agreement with the stored answers >= 0.90 AND F2 and F3 (as in S4) still hold when the re-query answers replace the stored ones on those turns. Raw answer = the arm's own answer before combining (UNSURE included).
S6  Speaker count (addendum H): scored only once >= 20 excerpts have a human count; below that the scorer reports n and "not scored". Arm 1 = production-side Nemotron count and pyannote count (two lines), arm 4 = L2. Each: exact_any (equals at least one human count, n) and exact_consensus (only windows where all human counts agree, n). Arm 3 Q3 secondary: a window counts as "third person detected" if any of its turns has q3 p >= 0.5, compared with human count >= 3 on windows where all humans agree on the side of 3; reported as a 2x2 with n. Arm 2 has no count output.
S7  Escalated share = DOUBTFUL turns / all scored turns. Cost and wall time = totals over the scored run divided by the audio hours of the scored windows (each window once). Cost uses prices.json (UNVERIFIED). Call seconds are serial-equivalent and, for arm 4, include clip cutting.

5 · Naming a voicenew

A person listens, then names. This is how the first room voiceprints get made, and it replaces sitting doctors down to record sentences. The reviewer must hear the voice before naming it — a voice with two people merged into it will poison every later match if it is named blind.

Cardiology OPD · 24 August

four voices found across seven windows

VoiceSpoke forWindowsSampleAgreement
Voice A1h 12m7 of 7 0.91 Name this voice
Voice B21m4 of 7 0.88 Name this voice
Voice C9m2 of 7 0.71 Name this voice
Voice D3m1 of 7 0.52 · may be two people Do not name
Agreement is how well the pieces of a voice match each other. A low number means the voice is probably two people put together, and naming it would put one person's speech into another person's voiceprint. Voice D is blocked for that reason.

6 · A patient's set of voicesnew

Every voice around a consultation is kept, and nobody decides which one is the patient. The role column is worked out from how often each voice comes back and for how many different patients — nothing is asserted.

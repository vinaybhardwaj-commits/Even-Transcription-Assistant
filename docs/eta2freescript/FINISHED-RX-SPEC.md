# FINISHED-RX-SPEC: the finished-prescription contract for ETA2Freescript artefacts
Version eta.freescript.v0.3, proposal, 2026-10-12. Author Veda, for EvenScribe Designer. Scored by analysis/RUBRIC-v1.md (section 6 here is the same rule set).
A finished prescription is an artefact a doctor can accept with taps alone: every field Pulse needs is either filled from the room with evidence, or explicitly handed to the doctor as completion_required. Nothing is guessed.

## 1. Envelope (R required, C conditional, O optional)
- schema_version: const "eta.freescript.v0.3" (R)
- artifact_id: uuid (R)
- supersedes: uuid|null (R): previous artefact for the same consult and generator
- created_at: ISO-8601 with offset (R)
- generator.{name,version,code_sha256,prompt_sha256,model,model_provider,zdr,temperature,run_id} (R): model_provider must be "aws-bedrock" and model must contain anthropic.claude (phi_guard); run_id is new, used by the scorer to de-duplicate
- encounter.{eta_encounter_id,room,window_start,window_end,visit_date_ist,languages,transcript{segment_count,diarised,english_source,speaker_source,role_track}} (R); visit_date_ist YYYY-MM-DD is new and anchors follow-up dates
- encounter.doctor.specialty (O)
- pulse_link.{type_of_prescription,consult_uid,prescription_uid,link_status,link_basis,draft_exists} (R); type_of_prescription = "HOSPITAL_GP"
- gp_extraction: GPLlmExtraction (section 2) (R): only items with provenance heard_in_room and a doctor decision
- provenance[]: ProvenanceItem (section 3) (R): exactly one per gp_extraction list item and per scalar field; also covers every kept-out item
- slots[]: PickDrugSlot (section 4) (R, may be empty): replaces the [regimen_slot] text items
- completion_required[]: CompletionItem (3.4) (R, may be empty)
- room_facts: {allergies[], vitals[]} (R, may be empty): heard-in-room allergies and vitals, review-only
- clip_boundary (R, as in v0.2); note_text (R): 11 FreeScript headings, G5/G6/C4 unchanged

## 2. gp_extraction (from pulsepb/parse_prescription.proto). Proto3 has no required keys and Pulse is non-strict; the R/C/O is ETA's contract. Unknown keys are silently dropped by wire.JsonUnmarshal, so ETA must not add keys inside gp_extraction.
2.1 Top level: examination (string, C: only physical findings stated aloud; otherwise omit and raise exam_missing; never a diagnosis); general_advice (O: doctor-spoken only, at most 5 items / 300 chars, '; '-joined, no colons); presenting_complaints (GPComplaint, C: required when any symptom or diagnosis was heard); plan_of_management (GPPlanOfManagement, C: required when any plan, conditional order or procedure was heard); medicines[] (LlmMedicine, O: named drugs, heard_in_room, doctor decision, status prescribed or continued); further_investigations[] (O: order_status = ordered only); comorbidities[] (O: current chronic conditions stated aloud; history goes to symptoms as "Hx - ..."); follow_up (LlmFollowUp, C: required when any follow-up statement was heard); referrals[] (O: doctor refers this patient, department name only).
2.2 GPComplaint: symptoms (C: what the patient reports plus duration; history as "Hx - ..."; no social or place detail unless clinically relevant); diagnoses[] (C: at least one when the doctor stated or clearly implied an assessment; at most 1 primary + 3 secondary); diagnoses[i].diagnosis_or_impression (R, standard clinical name, HTN -> Hypertension); is_differential_diagnosis (R, true for likely/possibly/suspected/rule out); raw_name (R, as said); aliases[] (O, at most 3). Order matters: diagnoses[0] is primary, the condition the plan treats; provenance carries is_primary true and icd10 {code, confidence}. Primary-diagnosis rule: when the doctor names a structural or functional label and also the symptom, the label is primary (DNS over nasal obstruction; functional dyspepsia over acidity). A symptom is never a separate diagnosis unless the doctor calls it one.
2.3 GPPlanOfManagement: management_plan (C: plan as said plus conditional orders as "X if Y", no colons); requires_surgery_or_procedure (bool, R when any procedure provenance exists): true iff at least one surgery_or_procedure_recommendations[] entry exists, never true without one; surgery_or_procedure_recommendations[] (C): one entry per procedure the doctor advised or offered for this patient, including an elective option the doctor recommends and leaves to the patient, and an ordered endoscopy or biopsy; [i].name (R, standard name, lap chole -> Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy); [i].raw_name (R); [i].aliases[] (O, at most 3); [i].scheduled_date (O, only if said, >= visit_date_ist); [i].notes (O: laterality, approach, conditions if said). Not a recommendation: "surgery may be needed later if not better" is conditional and goes to management_plan text plus completion_required{kind:surgery_confirm}; a past procedure goes to history; a procedure the patient asks about with no doctor advice is discussed_only. Pulse sets RequiresSurgeryOrProcedure from len(surgeries)>0 || pom.requires_surgery_or_procedure (px_mapper_hospital_gp.go ~:347); v2 sets neither today, so all signed surgery Rx's were missed.
2.4 LlmMedicine: name (R: generic or brand plus strength when strength is heard_in_room, e.g. "Paracetamol 1000 mg"; no Tab/Cap/Syp/Inj prefix; Pulse resolves name against the catalogue and takes Strength from the matched SKU); raw_name (R, as heard, mangled spelling kept); aliases[] (O, at most 3: generic for a brand or brands for a generic); frequency (C: only if heard, OD/BD/TDS/QID/HS/SOS/1-0-1; otherwise omit and raise dose_unstated); duration (C: only if heard; otherwise omit and raise dose_unstated); instructions (O: timing or route if heard).
2.5 LlmFurtherInvestigation: name (R, catalogue name, CBC -> Complete Blood Count); raw_name (R, as said; keeps panel intent); aliases[] (O, at most 3); scheduled_date (O, only if said, >= visit date); urgent (O, true only if urgency was said); instruction (O: fasting, morning sample, bring report, if said). Conditional, already_done, asked_only and crosstalk items are not sent; they stay in provenance only.
2.6 comorbidities[i].name/raw_name/aliases[] (R/R/O); comorbidities[i].duration (O, {value:int 1..1000, unit}); unit enum evenpb.DurationUnit DAYS=1, WEEKS=2, MONTHS=3, YEARS=4, HOURS=5 (spec proposes emitting YEARS directly; see DECISION above: default stays MONTHS x12 behind ETA_FREESCRIPT_EMIT_YEARS); comorbidities[i].notes (O, never duration or filler); referrals[i].speciality (R, department, never a doctor's name); referrals[i].notes (O, reason if said).
2.7 LlmFollowUp. Pulse drops the follow-up type when it is UNKNOWN (px_mapper_hospital_gp.go :640), so any follow-up ETA sends must carry a type. no_follow_up_needed (O: true only when the doctor says so; then every other follow_up key MUST be absent); followup_type (R when follow_up is present and no_follow_up_needed is not true): FOLLOW_UP_WITH_REPORTS=1, FOLLOW_UP_WITH_REFERRAL_PRESCRIPTION=2, FOLLOW_UP_WITH_REPORT_AND_REFERRAL_PRESCRIPTION=3, MANDATORY_FOLLOW_UP=4, IF_REQUIRED=5, never UNKNOWN; typing: with reports -> 1, referral -> 2, both -> 3, condition only ("come if worse") -> 5, otherwise 4. followup_date (R when type != IF_REQUIRED and an interval or date was heard): visit_date_ist + spoken interval, or the spoken date resolved to the next occurrence of a spoken weekday; must be >= visit date and <= visit + 2 years (normalizeLlmDate); if type != IF_REQUIRED and no interval was heard, omit the date and raise follow_up_unstated. follow_up_instructions (O: the spoken interval normalised, e.g. "Review after 3 months with lipid report", no colons). Not in the proto but required in provenance: interval {value, unit} as heard. If nothing about follow-up was heard, follow_up is omitted and completion_required{kind:follow_up_missing} is raised; never invent a default.

## 3. Provenance
3.1 evidence_state on every provenance item and every regimen part inside a medicine or slot: heard_in_room (spoken on tape; allowed in gp_extraction if status in {prescribed, continued} and a doctor decision exists; MUST carry at least one evidence[] span with quote, segment_ref, t0, t1, speaker, speaker_source); inferred (the model's clinical reading, not said; NOT allowed in gp_extraction, json_pointer null unless the doctor confirms in review; MUST carry basis_text and supporting spans if any; confidence <= 0.6); not_said_in_room (a field Pulse needs that the tape doesn't supply; not allowed in gp_extraction; MUST have a completion_required entry).
3.2 ProvenanceItem = { item_id, kind in {medicine, diagnosis, investigation, procedure, comorbidity, symptoms, examination, management_plan, advice, follow_up, referral, allergy, vital}, text, names[] (order kinds, at most 6), status in {prescribed, continued, stopped, not_given, discussed_only, conditional, already_done, asked_only, drug_history}, evidence_state, negated: bool, decision: {turn:int, speaker in {doctor, patient, attendant, staff, other, unknown}, quote} | null (REQUIRED for any item in gp_extraction), evidence: [{segment_ref:"<consult>:<turn>", t0:ISO, t1:ISO, speaker, speaker_source in {role_track, diarisation, model_inferred}, quote, quote_native?, lang}], confidence 0..1, json_pointer: RFC6901 | null, flags[], medicine?: {generic?, brand_as_heard?, strength:{value?, unit?, evidence_state, source in {doctor, patient, attendant, label}, as_heard?}, frequency:{text?, evidence_state}, duration:{text?, evidence_state}, timing:{text?, evidence_state}, explicit_continue: bool}, diagnosis?: {is_primary: bool, icd10:{code, confidence}, doctor_named: bool}, investigation?: {order_status, panel_hint?}, procedure?: {advised_kind in {advised, offered_elective, ordered, conditional}}, follow_up?: {interval:{value, unit} | null, said_text} }
3.3 Speaker: with a REB role track, speaker_source = role_track; without one, model_inferred plus the speaker_uncertain flag when role confidence < 0.7 or the cited speakers are mixed. An order whose decision turn was spoken by a non-doctor is never sent.
3.4 completion_required[] entries {kind, item_id, prompt, detail?}; item_id names a kept-out provenance item or a slot. Kinds: pick_drug (a slot exists); dose_unstated (a sent medicine lacks strength, frequency or duration); drug_history (a patient-reported current drug not addressed by the doctor: "Continue X?"); exam_missing; follow_up_unstated (follow-up said with no interval or date); follow_up_missing; surgery_confirm (surgery discussed conditionally); inferred_confirm (an inferred order or diagnosis offered for one-tap confirmation); allergy_check (an allergy was heard); boundary_review (the clip may contain another patient's consult).

## 4. Pick-drug slots. When the doctor prescribes without naming the drug ("one tablet before breakfast for 2 weeks", "apply this gel twice a day", "a syrup three times after food", "I've written a painkiller", "statin 10"), emit a slot, never a guessed name. Class words (statin, antibiotic, painkiller, PPI, medicine for acidity) are class, not name.
PickDrugSlot = { slot_id, form:{text?, evidence_state}, frequency:{text?, evidence_state}, duration:{text?, evidence_state}, timing:{text?, evidence_state}, class:{text?, evidence_state}, indication:{text?, evidence_state}, strength:{value?, unit?, evidence_state}, decision:{turn, speaker, quote}, evidence[], confidence <= 0.6, json_pointer:null }. Each slot raises completion_required{kind:"pick_drug", item_id:slot_id, prompt:"Name this drug", detail:"<form> <frequency> x <duration> <timing> (<class>, for <indication>)"}. One slot per distinct unnamed drug. Absent parts are evidence_state not_said_in_room, never null strings like "None".

## 5. Dose and regimen rules
1. The doctor's spoken strength wins. A strength or brand number spoken by the patient or attendant ("I take Dolo 650") never sets strength when the doctor states another ("paracetamol 1 g"): name = "Paracetamol 1000 mg", strength.source = doctor, the patient value goes in strength.as_heard with flag doctor_dose_precedence. A patient-only strength is not_said_in_room for the order, raises dose_unstated, and the patient value stays visible.
2. Unsaid regimen parts are completion_required: strength, frequency or duration not heard means that part is omitted from medicines[i], its state is not_said_in_room, and dose_unstated is raised.
3. Never infer a dose: every number in medicines[i].name/frequency/duration, and in a slot's frequency/duration/strength, must appear in a cited evidence span of that item (English and Hindi number words count; guard.py numeric support).
4. Stopped, negated and discussed-only drugs are never sent: status stopped/not_given/discussed_only/drug_history or negated true -> json_pointer null; none of the item's names[] may appear in medicines[] or the Medication section of note_text (G3, G6, C4); "Stop X" is written in management_plan.
5. "Continue" needs an explicit statement: status continued requires medicine.explicit_continue true and a doctor decision quote with a continuation cue (continue, keep taking, same tablet, carry on with). A patient-reported drug with no such statement is drug_history, raises "Continue X?" and is never sent.

## 6. Match rules against the signed Rx (scorer contract, same as RUBRIC-v1 section 2)
1. Medicines at molecule level: Rx catalogue generic_name vs FS name/raw_name/aliases through the catalogue plus the curated brand-generic map. 2. Combination brands expanded into components; grades full (same molecule set, 1.0), partial (at least one shared active ingredient, |FS∩Rx|/|Rx|), class (same ATC-4, judge-confirmed, 0.5). 3. Strength exact after unit normalisation; frequency as doses per day plus PRN flag; duration within max(1 day, 15%); a part ETA marked not_said_in_room scores fs_unstated (half credit), never mismatch. 4. One slot covers at most one Rx drug (slot_capture); its regimen parts are scored against that drug. 5. Investigations via the synonym map (CBC = Hemogram, RBS = GRBS, TMT = treadmill/exercise ECG, PTA = pure tone audiogram ...) and the panel map (LFT, lipid, thyroid, hospital packages, USG with PVR); FS ⊆ Rx panel = match, recall credit = covered/panel size. 6. Diagnoses at ICD-10 category level (first 3 chars) using provenance icd10.code and Rx diagnosis_icd_codes; primary scored separately; extra FS secondaries are S1. 7. Follow-up interval tolerance: <=14 d ±2 d; 15-60 d ±25%; >60 d ±30%; type compared exactly; an Rx date 1-3 days out with no instruction and no spoken interval is a suspected Pulse default, excluded and logged as Pulse-side. 8. Surgery/procedure flag scored as sensitivity and specificity; names match by catalogue id or the judge. 9. Recall uses spoken items only; Rx items with said_level = silent are reported as never_spoken_rate, never as FS misses.

## 7. Validator MUST rules for PR #41 (tools/eta2freescript/validate.py). These run after the existing schema check, G1-G6 (on the v0.1 projection) and C1-C4. Paths are RFC 6901 from the artefact root; P(x) = the provenance item whose json_pointer equals x. Any failure blocks the write, except where marked WARN.
Envelope: FRX-E1 /schema_version == "eta.freescript.v0.3", /generator/model contains anthropic.claude, /generator/model_provider == "aws-bedrock". FRX-E2 /encounter/visit_date_ist is a valid date equal to the IST date of /encounter/window_start. FRX-E3 no string in /gp_extraction, /note_text, /provenance/*/text or /slots matches the repo identity patterns (phone, Aadhaar, email, "Dr <Name>", person-name field).
Provenance: FRX-P1 every list item under /gp_extraction/{medicines, further_investigations, comorbidities, referrals, presenting_complaints/diagnoses, plan_of_management/surgery_or_procedure_recommendations}/i has exactly one P(path) of matching kind (extends G2 to procedures). FRX-P2 every P(x) with non-null json_pointer has evidence_state heard_in_room, status in {prescribed, continued}, negated false, decision != null; for order kinds (medicine, investigation, procedure, referral) decision.speaker == doctor, or unknown with flag speaker_uncertain. FRX-P3 every heard_in_room item has at least one evidence[k] with segment_ref matching ^[^:]+:\d+$ and turn index < /encounter/transcript/segment_count, t0 <= t1 both inside [window_start, window_end], speaker in enum, non-empty quote; decision.turn is one of the cited turns. FRX-P4 every inferred item has json_pointer null and confidence <= 0.6, and appears in /completion_required with kind inferred_confirm if it is an order or diagnosis. FRX-P5 every not_said_in_room part (medicine strength/frequency/duration, slot parts, follow_up date) is absent from /gp_extraction and covered by a completion_required entry whose item_id is the owning item or slot.
Medicines: FRX-M1 /gp_extraction/medicines/i/name non-empty, not matching ^(tab|tablet|cap|capsule|syp|syrup|inj|injection)\b (case-insensitive), not a class word (statin, antibiotic, painkiller, PPI, antacid, vitamin, medicine, tablet, syrup, cream, gel, drops). FRX-M2 if P(/medicines/i).medicine.strength.evidence_state == heard_in_room then name contains that value and unit; otherwise name contains no strength number and a completion_required{kind:dose_unstated} names the item. FRX-M3 every digit or number word in /medicines/i/{name,frequency,duration} and /slots/j/{frequency,duration,strength} occurs in that item's cited evidence quotes. FRX-M4 if strength.source in {patient, attendant, label} and a doctor-sourced strength exists in the same item's evidence, the doctor's value is in name and as_heard holds the other; flag doctor_dose_precedence. FRX-M5 frequency or duration present implies the matching medicine.{frequency|duration}.evidence_state == heard_in_room; absent implies dose_unstated raised. FRX-M6 status continued implies explicit_continue true and decision.quote matches /\b(continue|keep (taking|using)|same (tablet|medicine)|carry on)\b/i or the Hindi equivalents in guard.py. FRX-M7 for every item with status in {stopped, not_given, discussed_only, drug_history} or negated true: json_pointer null and no names[] entry matches any /medicines/* name, raw_name or alias (normalised, brand-generic aware). FRX-M8 (WARN) named medicine with empty aliases and no catalogue hit -> unresolved_name.
Slots: FRX-S1 every /slots/j has json_pointer null, no drug name in any field, and at least one of {form, frequency, duration, timing, class, indication, strength} heard_in_room. FRX-S2 every slot_id appears exactly once in /completion_required with kind pick_drug. FRX-S3 no slot field value is the literal "None", "null" or "".
Investigations/referrals/advice: FRX-I1 P(/further_investigations/i).investigation.order_status == ordered; conditional/already_done/asked_only items have json_pointer null. FRX-I2 scheduled_date present implies ISO date >= visit_date_ist plus heard evidence; urgent true implies the evidence quote contains an urgency cue. FRX-R1 /referrals/i/speciality does not match \bDr\.?\b or a person name, and P(...) has for_this_patient true. FRX-A1 general_advice <= 300 chars and <= 5 '; '-separated items, each heard_in_room with decision.speaker doctor. FRX-A2 if /room_facts/allergies non-empty, no /medicines/* molecule shares a drug class with a stated allergen, and completion_required{kind:allergy_check} exists.
Diagnoses: FRX-D1 if diagnoses non-empty, exactly one P(.../diagnoses/i) has is_primary true and it is i == 0. FRX-D2 icd10.code when present matches ^[A-Z][0-9]{2}(\.[0-9A-Z]{1,4})?$ with confidence in [0,1], and the code does not appear inside diagnosis_or_impression. FRX-D3 at most 4 diagnoses; a symptom-like secondary has doctor_named true.
Surgery: FRX-PR1 requires_surgery_or_procedure == true iff surgery_or_procedure_recommendations non-empty. FRX-PR2 any procedure item with status prescribed and advised_kind in {advised, offered_elective, ordered} has a non-null json_pointer into surgery_or_procedure_recommendations and PR1 holds; a conditional procedure has json_pointer null, its text appears in management_plan, and completion_required{kind:surgery_confirm} exists. FRX-PR3 recommendations/i/{name, raw_name} non-empty; scheduled_date if present is ISO and >= visit date.
Follow-up: FRX-F1 follow_up present and no_follow_up_needed != true implies followup_type is one of the 5 non-UNKNOWN names. FRX-F2 type != IF_REQUIRED and (interval != null or a date was heard) implies followup_date present, valid, in [visit_date_ist, visit_date_ist + 730 d]; with a heard interval the date equals visit_date_ist + interval (±1 day for month ends). FRX-F3 no_follow_up_needed true implies followup_type, followup_date, follow_up_instructions all absent. FRX-F4 no follow-up provenance implies /gp_extraction/follow_up absent and completion_required{kind:follow_up_missing} exists.
Completion/note: FRX-C1 every completion_required item_id resolves to a provenance item with json_pointer null, or to a slot; kind in the 3.4 enum. FRX-C2 G5, G6, C4 hold on note_text; the Surgery or Procedure section has exactly one line per recommendation entry and names it.

T8 [S0] "I'm giving you a syrup, two spoons three times after food, for two weeks." T10 [S0] "If it's not better in two weeks we'll do an endoscopy." T11 [S0] "Come back if the burning continues." ## 9. Open questions for Pulse: (1) YEARS in LlmDuration.unit (w38 v0.1 says no, Pulse code says yes); (2) GPLlmExtraction has no ICD field, so icd10 stays in provenance; should Pulse add LlmDiagnosis.icd10_code? (3) Build overwrites examination/symptoms/plan/advice, so a structured ingest must merge with doctor-typed content first; (4) a one-tap "Name this drug" slots UI pre-filtered by form/class/indication is the main recall lever (80% of signed drugs never named aloud in v2); (5) is there a Pulse target for allergies and vitals, or is room_facts review-only?

## Decision (ETA, 2026-10-12) — open question 1

YEARS stays MONTHS×12 by default. `ETA_FREESCRIPT_EMIT_YEARS` unset or `0` keeps that conversion, because it passes both the vendored w38 schema and Pulse. `ETA_FREESCRIPT_EMIT_YEARS=1` emits YEARS when Pulse confirms. An unrecognised value raises. `to_v01` rewrites a representable YEARS value to MONTHS×12 so the w38 validator still passes. This decision does not change the proposal text above.

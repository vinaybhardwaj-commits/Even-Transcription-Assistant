EvenScribe Room Recorder — third-party software notices
=======================================================

This application bundles two third-party components inside
`EvenScribe Room Recorder.app/Contents/Helpers/`. Both are used unmodified,
as separate executables invoked by the application; neither is linked into
EvenScribe's own binaries.

The exact upstream archives, their versions and their verified SHA-256
checksums are recorded in `Contents/Resources/Licenses/source-lock.json`,
which is reproduced from the build's pinned source lock. That file is the
authoritative statement of what was built.
